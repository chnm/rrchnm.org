title: CHNM to build database for National Museum of American Jewish History
---

CHNM has been awarded a contract build a custom collections management system for the [National Museum of American Jewish History](http://www.nmajh.org/) in Philadelphia. The new system will allow NMAJH researchers working at museums and historical societies around the nation to upload and organize images and information about collections of American Judaica via a web-based interface. NMAJH will use the system to create comprehensive database of artifacts from American Jewish history in preparation for a planned new permanent exhibition.
