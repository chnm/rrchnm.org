title: Echo exhibit reviewed in The Public Historian
---

[A Thin Blue Line](http://history.nih.gov/exhibits/thinblueline/), CHNM's online exhibition documenting the history of the pregnancy test kit is the subject of a website review published in the most recent issue of *The Public Historian*, the journal of the National Council on Public History. A Thin Blue Line is a collaboration between CHNM's [Echo](http://echo.gmu.edu/) project and the National Institutes of Health and features an [online survey](http://echo.gmu.edu/nih/survey.php) of visitor's experiences with home pregancy tests.
Click [here](http://chnm.gmu.edu/old_news/archives/public_history_tbl_review.pdf) to read the review.
