On Tuesday, January 10, Roy Rosenzweig and Dan Cohen will discuss their new book, [Digital History: A Guide to Gathering, Preserving, and Presenting the Past on the Web](http://chnm.gmu.edu/digitalhistory/) on NPR and WAMU's [The Kojo Nnamdi Show](http://www.wamu.org/programs/kn/index.php). Rosenzweig and Cohen will take questions live from noon to 1 PM EST, and the entire show will be syndicated via iTunes and other outlets as part of [WAMU's podcast series](http://www.wamu.org/help/podcasts.php).
