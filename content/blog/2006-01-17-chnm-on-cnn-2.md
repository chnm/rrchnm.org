On Friday, January 13, CNN's "The Situation Room with Wolf Blitzer" featured CHNM's [Hurricane Digital Memory Bank](http://hurricanearchive.org/). Internet reporter, Jacki Schechner, [enthusiastically highlighted](http://transcripts.cnn.com/TRANSCRIPTS/0601/13/sitroom.01.html) the Hurricane Digital Memory Bank's efforts to collect the stories and memories of the 2005 hurricane season.
