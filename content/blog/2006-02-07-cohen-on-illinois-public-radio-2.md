On Friday, February 3, CHNM's Director of Research Projects, [Dan Cohen](http://www.dancohen.org/) appeared on Illinois Public Radio's [Focus 580](http://www.will.uiuc.edu/am/focus/default.htm) to discuss his recent book, [Digital History](http://chnm.gmu.edu/digitalhistory/) and other topics in history and new media. The entire interview is now available as [an MP3 file](http://www.will.uiuc.edu/willmp3/focus060203a.mp3) from WILL's website.
