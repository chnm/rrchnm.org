[KOLE](http://www.foxnews1340.com/index.asp) radio in Beaumont, TX will feature CHNM's [Hurricane Digital Memory Bank](http://hurricanearchive.org/) on Friday, February 10 between 3 and 4 pm (CST). Fox Forum host Dan Gresham will interview Hurricane Digital Memory Bank Project Manager, Sheila Brennan, and will take calls during that time. Listeners may tune into 1340 or 1380 AM, or [listen to a streamed broadcast](http://www.foxnews1340.com/streaming_1340.asp) online.
