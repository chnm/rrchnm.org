title: Katrina's Jewish Voices heard in CHNM partner site
---

To preserve the Jewish experience of Hurricane Katrina, the [Jewish Women's Archive](http://jwa.org/) has announced the launch of [Katrina's Jewish Voices](http://katrina.jwa.org/), an online collecting project and digital archive. Employing technologies and techniques developed at CHNM and in collaboration with CHNM's [Hurricane Digital Memory Bank](http://hurricanearchive.org/), Katrina's Jewish Voices allows members of the Jewish community in New Orleans and across the country to contribute their stories and photographs. The collection currently stands at nearly 200 images, emails, documents, and other digital objects related to the Jewish experience of Hurricane Katrina and is growing daily.
Since 1995, the Jewish Women's Archive has worked to uncover, chronicle, and transmit the rich history of American Jewish women.
