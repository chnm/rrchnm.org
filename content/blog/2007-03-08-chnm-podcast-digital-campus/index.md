![buttonlogo04.jpg](http://chnm.gmu.edu/old_news//buttonlogo04.jpg)CHNM is happy to announce the launch of a new podcast, *Digital Campus*, now available from [iTunes](itpc://feeds.feedburner.com/digitalcampus) and <http://digitalcampus.tv>
The biweekly roundtable will discuss how digital media and technology are affecting learning, teaching, and scholarship at colleges, universities, libraries, and museums. [Our inaugural episode](http://digitalcampus.tv/podcasts/dc_ep01_wikipedia.mp3 "Episode 01-Wikipedia: Friend or Foe?") features CHNM Director of Research Projects, [Dan Cohen](http://www.dancohen.org), CHNM Associate Director, [Mills Kelly](http://www.edwired.org), and CHNM Assistant Director, [Tom Scheinfeldt](http://www.foundhistory.org) discussing the controversy over whether [Wikipedia](http://wikipedia.org) is a useful or problematic resource for student, the launch of [Windows Vista](http://www.microsoft.com/windows/products/windowsvista/default.mspx), the rise of [Google Docs](http://docs.google.com) as an alternative to MS Word, and recent stories about [Blackboard](http://www.blackboard.com)’s patents and their social bookmarking site, [Scholar.com](http://scholar.com). And at the end of the podcast, we share links to the best free [wiki](http://pbwiki.com) [software](http://wetpaint.com) and sites on digital [maps](http://www.worldmapper.org) and [books](http://www.archive.org/details/texts).
Subscribe to *Digital Campus* via [iTunes](itpc://feeds.feedburner.com/digitalcampus) or at <http://digitalcampus.tv>
