title: 'Roy Rosenzweig: Memorial Events'
---

Several memorial events are being planned to celebrate the life and work of CHNM's beloved late director, Roy Rosenzweig, who passed away earlier this month after a valiant struggle with cancer. Details will be posted at [thanksroy.org/memorialevents](http://thanksroy.org/memorialevents), where friends, colleagues, and admirers may also post memories, stories, tributes, photos and other materials in celebration of Roy.
