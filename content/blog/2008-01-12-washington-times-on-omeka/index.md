title: Washington Times on Omeka
---

[CHNM](http://chnm.gmu.edu) and its new platform for publishing collections and exhibits online, [Omeka](http://omeka.org), were featured this week in the [Washington Times](http://www.washingtontimes.com/apps/pbcs.dll/article?AID=/20080110/FAMILY/236719400/).
Describing CHNM as being "at the forefront of the new wave of collecting history," the article went on to highlight the [September 11 Digital Archive](http://911digitalarchive.org), the [Hurricane Digital Memory Bank](http://hurricanearchive.org), and [History Matters](http://historymatters.gmu.edu/). Virginia Tech's [April 16 Archive](http://april16archive.org/), which runs on the Omeka platform, was also mentioned.
