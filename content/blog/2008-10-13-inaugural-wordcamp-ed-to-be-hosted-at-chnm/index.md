title: Inaugural WordCamp Ed to be hosted at CHNM
---

On Saturday, November 22, 2008, CHNM will host the very first [WordCamp Ed](http://dc2008.wordcamped.org/), a [WordCamp](http://central.wordcamp.org/) conference focused entirely on educational uses of the [WordPress](http://wordpress.org/) blogging and content management software in schools and universities. Organized in partnership with the [Center for New Designs in Learning and Scholarship](http://cndls.georgetown.edu/) at Georgetown University, the one-day event will feature a morning of pre-planned speakers and a barcamp-style afternoon of smaller discussion sessions. [Registration](http://dc2008.wordcamped.org/wp-login.php?action=register) is free at the WordCamp Ed blog.
WordCamp Ed builds on the success of last spring's [THATCamp](http://thatcamp.org), reinforcing CHNM's growing importance as a gathering place for the educational and cultural technology communities.
