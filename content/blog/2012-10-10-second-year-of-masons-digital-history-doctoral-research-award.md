title: Second Year of Mason's Digital History Doctoral Research Award
---

A reminder to potential doctoral students in history that [George Mason University](http://www.gmu.edu) and the [Roy Rosenzweig Center for History and New Media](http://chnm.gmu.edu) have Digital History Research Awards for students entering the [History and Art History](http://historyarthistory.gmu.edu) doctoral program. Students receiving these awards will get five years of fully funded studies, as follows: $20,000 research stipends in years 1 and 2; research assistantships at RRCHNM in years 3, 4, and 5. Awards include fulltime tuition waivers and student health insurance. For more information, contact Professor Cynthia A. Kierner (Director of the Ph.D. Program) at [ckierner@gmu.edu](mailto:ckierner@gmu.edu), or yours truly at [dcohen@gmu.edu](mailto:dcohen@gmu.edu). The deadline for applications is January 15, 2013.
