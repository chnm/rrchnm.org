The Roy Rosenzweig Center for History and New Media at George Mason University is pleased to announce *[Hidden in Plain Sight](http://edchnm.gmu.edu/hidden/)*, an online U.S. history course created for teachers with funding from the Virginia Department of Education. This Spring 2013 course may be taken for recertification points or for graduate credit.
[caption id="attachment\_1956" align="aligncenter" width="300"][![http://edchnm.gmu.edu/hidden](http://chnm.gmu.edu/wordpress/wp-content/files/2013/01/hips2-300x181.jpg)](http://chnm.gmu.edu/news/online-u-s-history-course-hidden-in-plain-sight-sp-2013/attachment/hips2/) http://edchnm.gmu.edu/hidden[/caption]
**45 Recertification Points**
Participants work through eight modules. In each module, requirements include writing a hypothesis, exploring historical context, completing a quiz, and reflecting on classroom applications. The cost is $40. [Register](http://edchnm.gmu.edu/hidden/recertification-course-registration) by January 16, 2013.
**3 Graduate Credits**
For graduate credit, teachers participate in a related course with eight modules. In each module, requirements include writing a hypothesis, exploring historical context, reading scholarly articles, completing a quiz, and reflecting on classroom applications. Participants will design and produce a lesson on the hidden history of a historical object as the final project. The cost is $800 for Virginia residents ($875 out-of-state). [Pre-register](http://edchnm.gmu.edu/hidden/graduate-course-registration) by January 16, 2013.
Visit the course [website](http://edchnm.gmu.edu/hidden) for more information.
