title: 'Applications Open for "Rebuilding the Portfolio: DH for Art Historians"'

Join us July 7-18, 2014 for *Rebuilding the Portfolio: DH for Art Historians*, a summer institute at the [Roy Rosenzweig Center for History in New Media](http://chnm.gmu.edu),supported by the [Getty Foundation](http://www.getty.edu/foundation/).
*Rebuilding the Portfolio: DH for Art Historians* is designed for 20 art historians, from different stages of their careers and from varied backgrounds, including faculty, curators, art librarians, and archivists who are eager to explore the digital turn in the humanities.
We seek applications from individuals who have had very limited or no training in using digital methods and tools, or in computing.
Take a peek at our proposed [schedule](http://arthistory2014.doingdh.org/schedule), and apply today. [Applications](http://arthistory2014.doingdh.org/applications/) will be open until March 15, 2014.
