title: Introducing the Proceedings of THATCamp!
---

[THATCamp](http://thatcamp.org "THATCamp"), The Humanities and Technology Camp, is an open, inexpensive meeting where humanists and technologists of all skill levels meet to learn and build together in sessions proposed on the spot: it is a well-known and popular global unconference. The *[Proceedings of THATCamp](http://proceedings.thatcamp.org/ "Proceedings of THATCamp")* is a wholly automatic collection of and portal to blog posts from around the [THATCamp website network](http://thatcamp.org/camps).
{{< figure src="http://proceedings.thatcamp.org/" alt="potc" caption="Proceedings of THATCamp" >}}
Because each THATCamp is organized independently and has an individual site on the THATCamp network, the *[Proceedings](http://proceedings.thatcamp.org/ "Proceedings of THATCamp")* offers an easy way to view all the activity happening at THATCamps [around the world](http://thatcamp.org/camps/). Posts are viewable by  [date](http://proceedings.thatcamp.org/dates/), by [topic](http://proceedings.thatcamp.org/topics/)**,** or by how often they have been “*[favorited](http://proceedings.thatcamp.org/ "Proceedings of THATCamp")*” by [THATCamp participants](http://thatcamp.org/people/) across the network. [Tweets](http://proceedings.thatcamp.org/twitter/) and [pictures](http://proceedings.thatcamp.org/pictures/) from the [Flickr User Group](https://www.flickr.com/groups/thatcamp/) also are aggregated in the *[Proceedings](http://proceedings.thatcamp.org/ "Proceedings of THATCamp")*.
The launch of the *[Proceedings](http://proceedings.thatcamp.org/ "Proceedings of THATCamp")* features the most favorited posts since April 2008. The time will be updated periodically, to feature the most favorited posts in the last quarter or trimester.
The *[Proceedings](http://proceedings.thatcamp.org/ "Proceedings of THATCamp")* utilizes the features of both [WordPress Multisite](http://codex.wordpress.org/Create_A_Network) and a customized version of [BuddyPress](http://buddypress.org) on the [THATCamp.org](http://thatcamp.org) site. Aggregation for the *[Proceedings](http://proceedings.thatcamp.org/ "Proceedings of THATCamp")* is made possible by the [WordPress MU Sitewide Tags](http://wordpress.org/plugins/wordpress-mu-sitewide-tags/) plugin, the [FeedWordPress](http://wordpress.org/plugins/feedwordpress/) plugin, and a custom plugin that relies on BuddyPress’s favoriting features.
The web developers for the *[Proceedings](http://proceedings.thatcamp.org/ "Proceedings of THATCamp")* were [Boone Gorges](http://thatcamp.org/people/boone-gorges) and [Aram Zucker-Scharff](http://thatcamp.org/people/azuckers), while [Kim Nguyen](http://thatcamp.org/people/design) designed the theme. [Amanda French](http://thatcamp.org/people/amandafrench), the THATCamp coordinator, served as chief editor and project manager. Congratulations to the whole team!
