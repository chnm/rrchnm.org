Sharon Leon is leading a workshop on Omeka S on February 28, from 10am-12pm, and delivering a talk entitled "[Complicating a “Great Man” Narrative of Digital History](http://cdsc.libraries.wsu.edu/public-lecture/complicating-a-great-man-narrative-of-digital-history/)," on February 28, 3.30-5.00pm, at the [Center for Digital Scholarship and Curation](https://cdsc.libraries.wsu.edu/events-and-news/join-us/) at Washington State University.
