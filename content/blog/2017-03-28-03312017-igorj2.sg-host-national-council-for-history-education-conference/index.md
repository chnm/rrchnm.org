RRCHNM Graduate Research Assistant, Sara Collini, and Harper Park Middle School Civics teacher (and RRCHNM alum!), Jessica Kilday, will be at the [National Council for History Education annual conference](http://www.nche.net/details) in Atlanta from March 30 to April 1 to promote Eagle Eye Citizen, an interactive website that helps students learn about Congress and civic participation through explorations of Library of Congress primary sources.

Stop by booth #25 or plan to attend their conference presentation on Friday, March 31 at 2pm.
