{{< figure src="https://rrchnm.org/uncategorized/amboyna-conspiracy-trial-website-named-finalist-for-nsw-premiers-history-award/attachment/amboyna-5/" >}}The [Amboyna Conspiracy Trial](http://amboyna.org), an interactive teaching resource focused on one of the most famous legal cases of the early modern period, is a finalist for the Australian [NSW Premier’s History Awards](http://www.sl.nsw.gov.au/nsw-premiers-history-awards) [Multimedia History Prize](http://www.sl.nsw.gov.au/multimedia-history-prize).
Created by [Dr. Adam Clulow](http://profiles.arts.monash.edu.au/adam-clulow) ([Monash University](http://www.monash.edu), Australia) in collaboration with [RRCHNM](https://rrchnm.org) and the design team at [Big Yellow Taxi](http://bigyellowtaxi.com/), the [Amboyn](http://amboyna.org)[a Conspiracy Trial](http://amboyna.org) is part of RRCHNM’s [World History Matters](http://worldhistorymatters.org) portal offering rich resources for teaching and learning about world history.
Judges in the award competition [praised](http://www.sl.nsw.gov.au/amboyna-conspiracy-trial-adam-clulow) the “rich trove of digitised archival material” and the interactivity that turns visitors into “investigators, lawyers and jurors tasked with understanding historical events.”
The NSW Premier’s History Awards “assist in establishing values and standards in historical research and publication" encouraging broad audiences to "appreciate and learn from" historians. Winners will be announced at the [State Library of New South Wales](http://www.sl.nsw.gov.au) in Australia on September 1, 2017.
