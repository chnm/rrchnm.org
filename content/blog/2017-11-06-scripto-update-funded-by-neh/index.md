title: Scripto Update Funded by NEH
---

We are pleased to announce that the Omeka team has an opportunity to upgrade its popular Scripto tool with funding from the [National Endowment for the Humanities Office of Digital Humanities (NEH-ODH)](https://www.neh.gov/divisions/odh/grant-news/announcing-new-2017-odh-grant-awards). [Scripto](http://scripto.org) is a free, open-source tool used for collaborative online transcriptions of documents and multimedia files, originally funded by the NEH-ODH in 2009, used by many.
We are grateful for this opportunity to redesign Scripto as a module for Omeka S, and to generate new documentation and guidance that will assist other cultural heritage organizations in managing their own community transcription projects.
This work will coincide with the migration of the [Papers of the War Department](http://wardepartmentpapers.org) digital documentary edition to Omeka S with funding from the American Council of Learned Societies.
