There are four sessions involving RRCHNM on the program at the 132nd Annual Meeting of the American Historical Association in Washington DC:
[(1) Teaching Hidden History: Learning by Developing Digital Modules](https://aha.confex.com/aha/2018/webprogram/Session15939.html) (Saturday, 1/6/2018, 1.30PM-3.00PM) **Kelly Schrum & Nate Sleeter**
[(2) Arguing with Digital History: A Roundtable on Using Digital History to Make Arguments for Academic Audiences](https://aha.confex.com/aha/2018/webprogram/Session16534.html) (Saturday, 1/6/2018, 1.30PM-3.00PM) *Stephen Robertson & Lincoln Mullen**\* The white paper to be discussed in this session can be found [here](https://rrchnm.org/argument-white-paper/)*
[(3) Diplomacy in Action: Diplomacy Simulations in the Classroom](https://aha.confex.com/aha/2018/webprogram/Session16108.html) (Sunday, 1/7/2018, 9.00AM-10.30AM) **Gwen White**
[(4) Understanding Sacrifice: A Lens for Studying WW2 through Art, Science, Literature & History](https://aha.confex.com/aha/2018/webprogram/Session15862.html) (Sunday, 1/7/2018, 12.30PM-4.00PM) *Jennifer Rosenfeld & Christopher Hamner*
Also, on Friday, 1/5/2018, from 11.30AM-2.30PM, you can also find an RRCHNM table in the [Affiliated Societies Display](https://aha.confex.com/aha/2018/webprogram/Session16861.html), in the atrium outside the exhibit hall in the Marriott Wardman Park.
