Come see RRCHNM showcase [Eagle Eye Citizen](https://eagleeyecitizen.org) in the 2018 ED Games Expo at the Kennedy Center for the Performing Arts on Monday, January 8 from 10am-8pm!
See more than 100 learning games and technologies in action at the 5th annual [**ED Games Expo**](https://ies.ed.gov/sbir/EdGamesExpo.asp). Technologies at the Expo are for children and students age 3 to 18, and cover a range of topics in education and special education across STEM, reading and writing, social studies, and social and behavioral development.
The Expo is free and open to the public.
**Date/Time:** Monday, January 8 from 10am-8pm
**Location:** The John F. Kennedy Center for the Performing Arts, 2700 F St. NW, Washington, D.C.
Contact [Edward.Metz@ed.gov](mailto:Edward.Metz@ed.gov) with questions.
