Stephen Robertson will be part of a team working on [Promoting a Public Face for Scholarly Journals](https://trianglesci.org/2018/07/10/promoting-a-public-face-for-scholarly-journals/) at the Triangle Scholarly Communications Institute from October 7-11, 2018. Together with Lisa Brady, Liz Covart, Seth Denbo, Robert Greene II, and Catherine Halley, he will be developing a guide to platforms and strategies for public engagement to disseminate online, and present at the journal editors’ breakfast at the 2019 American Historical Association conference.
