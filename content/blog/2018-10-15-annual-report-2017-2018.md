![](/media/2018/10/RRCHNM_AR_2018_Page_1-791x1024.jpg)Our short annual report has recently been mailed out to RRCHNM’s friends and supporters. Produced in collaboration with the Development Office of College of Humanities and Social Sciences, the annual report describes project launches and grants for new projects in the academic year 2017-2018. In it you can find brief descriptions of five of those new projects, and of a busy year of teaching, training, and professional development.  A report on the Center’s endowment completes the document.
You can download a copy of the Annual Report [here.](/media/2018/10/RRCHNM_AR_2018.pdf)
