RRCHNM is hosting the 2019 Current Research in Digital History conference on March 9, 2019, at the Arlington campus of George Mason University. The [conference program](https://crdh.rrchnm.org/conference/2019/) features short presentations that offer historical arguments and interpretations rather than showcase digital projects. This year’s conference features two panels sponsored by the [African American Intellectual History Society](https://www.aaihs.org/) and the [Colored Conventions Project](http://coloredconventions.org/). Presentations are peer-reviewed and published in an [online publication](https://crdh.rrchnm.org/volume/2018/) that accommodates dynamic visualizations and narrative.
