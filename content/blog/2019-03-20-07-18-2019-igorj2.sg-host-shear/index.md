Greta Swain and Jordan Bratt will be presenting as part of the roundtable "Visualizing Democracy: Voting, Political Parties, and the [Mapping Early Americans Elections](http://earlyamericanelections.org/) Project" at the annual meeting of the [Society for Historians of the Early American Republic (SHEAR)](http://www.shear.org/annual-meeting/)  in Cambridge,MA, on July 19-21, 2019.
