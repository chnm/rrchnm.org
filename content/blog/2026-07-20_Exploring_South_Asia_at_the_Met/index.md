Deepthi Murali's article, **"South Asia at The Met,"** demonstrates how museum data can uncover stories that extend beyond the objects themselves. By analyzing The Metropolitan Museum of Art's open-access collection data, Murali explores how the museum's South Asian collection has grown over time and what collection metadata can reveal about institutional collecting practices.

Rather than focusing on individual artworks, the article examines acquisition records, donor information, object classifications, and display data to identify broader trends. Murali highlights major periods of collecting, the influence of significant donors, and the reality that only a small percentage of South Asian objects are currently on public display.

The project showcases the value of combining digital humanities methods with open museum data to better understand art history, museum collections, and the decisions that shape what audiences experience in galleries.
