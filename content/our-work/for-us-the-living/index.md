# For Us The Living

For Us the Living: Learning from the Stories of the Alexandria National Cemetery is a series of online educational modules designed to teach high school students American history through the lens of national cemeteries.
The Roy Rosenzweig Center for History and New Media at George Mason University, in partnership with Alexandria County Public Schools, developed For Us the Living as a part of the Veterans Legacy Program, a project supported by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) National Cemetery Administration (NCA).
