# Zotero

Zotero is a free, easy-to-use tool to help you collect, organize, cite, and share research. Created at RRCHNM in 2006, Zotero and is used by millions of people every day all around the world, making it one of the most successful pieces of software ever built for the humanities. In 2016, RRCHNM shifted Omeka to the Corporation for Digital Scholarship, a non-profit corporation created by several faculty from RRCHNM (and others) in 2009. CDS has been financially responsible for this very successful software since 2013.
