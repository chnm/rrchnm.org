\nCaroline Greer is a fourth-year Ph.D. student in the History Department and a graduate affiliate of RRCHNM. Previously, she worked as the project manager of American Religious Ecologies and has been a digital history fellow at the University of Luxembourg. She has a master’s degree in history from Auburn University and a bachelor’s degree in history from Louisiana State University. Her research focuses on gender and religion in the early American Republic.
