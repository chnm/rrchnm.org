Luz Adriana is a first year History Ph.D. student and Teaching Assistant at George Mason University. Luz Adriana received a Spanish Master’s degree from George Mason University. She received the 2021 Dean's Challenge Scholarship Award from the College of Humanities and Social Sciences for her graduate work on Colombian children’s literature and media of trauma in the context of the armed conflict and the framework of education for peace initiatives. In 2021 she received a diploma in Historical Memory from the Universidad de Antioquia in Colombia. Luz has presented papers at Georgetown University, the University of Maryland, Towson University and the 25th IRSCL Biennial Conference. She has also served as the Editor-in-chief for the Hispanic Culture Review, Mason's only bilingual journal. Her research interests include Latin American History, Peace Processes, Memory Studies and Historic Narrative.
