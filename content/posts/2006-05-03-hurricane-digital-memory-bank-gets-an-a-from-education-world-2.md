Stating that the "site is very well designed and interesting," [Education World](http://www.education-world.com/index.shtml) awarded CHNM's [Hurricane Digital Memory Bank](http://hurricanearchive.org/) an [A+ rating](http://www.education-world.com/awards/2006/r0406-28.shtml). It also recommended the project to students and teachers at nearly all grade levels, from early elementary to high school. Education World helps educators to integrate the Internet easily into the classroom by offering free resources and reviews through its website.
