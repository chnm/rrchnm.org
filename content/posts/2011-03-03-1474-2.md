Fairfax County Public Schools teachers explored the activities and case studies found within *[For Virginians: Government Matters](http://vagovernmentmatters.org/)* on March 1 at an inservice about state and local government. The day included presentations by Chairman Sharon Bulova, Delegate Scott Surovell, and former Senator Emilie Miller.
