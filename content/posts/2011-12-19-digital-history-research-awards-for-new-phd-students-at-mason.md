title: Digital History Research Awards for New PhD Students at Mason

---

[George Mason University](http://www.gmu.edu/) and the [Roy Rosenzweig Center for History and New Media](http://chnm.gmu.edu/) are pleased to announce Digital History Research Awards for students entering the [History and Art History](http://historyarthistory.gmu.edu/) doctoral program in fall 2012. Students receiving these awards will get five years of fully funded studies, as follows: $20,000 research stipends in years 1 and 2; research assistantships at RRCHNM in years 3, 4, and 5. Awards include fulltime tuition waivers and student health insurance. For more information, contact Professor Cynthia A. Kierner (Director of the Ph.D. Program) at [ckierner@gmu.edu](mailto:ckierner@gmu.edu) or Professor Dan Cohen (Director, Roy Rosenzweig Center for History and New Media) at [dcohen@gmu.edu](mailto:dcohen@gmu.edu). The deadline for applications is January 15, 2012.
