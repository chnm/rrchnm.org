John Turner and Lincoln Mullen will be participating in a symposium hosted by St. Louis University from October 10 to 12 on "[Religion in Place: Spaces | Borders | Bodies](http://religioninplace.org/blog/fall-symposium/)." They will be discussing RRCHNM's recently begun project, *[American Religious Ecologies](https://rrchnm.org/news/igorj2.sg-host-to-digitize-the-1926-census-of-religious-bodies/)*.
