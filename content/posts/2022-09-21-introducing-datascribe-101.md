#### Fall workshops hosted by the DataScribe project team will further support user exploration of DataScribe 101's capabilities.

Earlier this year, RRCHNM released [DataScribe](https://datascribe.tech/), a structured transcription module for the [Omeka S platform](https://omeka.org/s/). This module enables scholars to identify the structure of the data within their sources, speed up the transcription of their sources, and reliably structure their transcriptions in a form amenable to computational analysis. Scholars can turn sources into tables of data stored as numbers, dates, categories, and more.

The latest release brings improvements to the module in addition to new [resources](https://datascribe.tech/resources/) for users to reference when using DataScribe for their own work. Resources available include [tutorials](https://datascribe.tech/resources/tutorials/) and [case studies](https://datascribe.tech/resources/casestudies/) that cover the applied use of DataScribe for large transcription projects including [American Religious Ecologies](https://religiousecologies.org/) and [Death by Numbers](https://deathbynumbers.org/).

For users interested in a more in depth review of how to use DataScribe, the DataScribe Project team is pleased to announce their Fall Workshops and Webinars series.
