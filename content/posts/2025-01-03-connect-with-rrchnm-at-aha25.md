Many RRCHNM-ers will be featured in sessions coming up this week at the American Historical Associations Annual Meeting. To connect with RRHCNM and learn more about our current projects, here is a list of those sessions.

## **Saturday, January 4**

##### [Digitizing Black History at HBCU’s: A Collaborative Public History Approach](https://aha.confex.com/aha/2025/webprogram/Session26272.html)

Bowery (Sheraton New York, Lower Level), 8:30am-10am

Chaired by Marion McGee of *the Smithsonian Institution, National Museum of African American History and Culture*, and a panel with Catiana Foster of *Tuskeegee University,* Timmia King of *RRCHNM and George Mason University,* Raymond (Garrad) Lee of *Jackson State University,* Barbara Twyman of *Florida A&M University,* Shyheim Williams of *Clark Atlanta University.*

## Sunday, January 5

##### [Unpacking the History of Higher Education in the United States](https://aha.confex.com/aha/2025/webprogram/Session26565.html)

Regent Room (New York Hilton, Second Floor), 8:30am-10am

Chaired by Nathan Sleeter and Kelly Schrum of *RRCHNM and George Mason University*, with presentations by Liza Bolitzer of *Kean University,* LaNesha DeBardelabon of *University of Washington, Seattle,* Carrie Kortegast of *Northern Illinois University,* Amy Pajewski of *West Chester University,* Chadrhyn Pedraza of *New Mexico State University.*

##### Poster Session 2 at Grand Ballroom (Grand Ballroom, New York Hilton, Third Floor), 10:30am-12:30pm

[A Religious Digital Revolution](https://aha.confex.com/aha/2025/webprogram/Paper39455.html) – Rachel Whyte

##### [History Podcast Pitch Party: New Ideas for New Podcast Series](https://aha.confex.com/aha/2025/webprogram/Session26238.html)

Gramercy West (New York Hilton, Second Floor), 1:30pm-3pm

Chaired by James Ambuske of *RRCHNM’s R2 Studios*

##### Proof Session: [Researching Race and Space in the Digital Era: African American Spatial Biographies](https://aha.confex.com/aha/2025/speakerscorner.cgi?username=171278&password=100686&EntryType=Person)

Sheraton New York, Gramercy 285, 3:30pm-5pm

Chaired by Andrea Catharina Mosterman of *University of New Orleans* with presentations by Laura Brannan Fretwell of *George Mason University, RRCHNM*, Anthony Guidone of *Radford University,* Great Swain of *Southern Methodist University.*

##### [Data-Driven Histories Showcase](https://aha.confex.com/aha/2025/webprogram/Session26466.html)

Regent room (New York Hilton, Second Floor), 3:30pm-5pm

Chaired by Lincoln Mullen, *RRCHNM, George Mason University*

## Monday, January 6

##### [Teaching Writing Workshop: Teaching Writing in the Age of AI](https://aha.confex.com/aha/2025/webprogram/Session26656.html)

Concourse A (New York Hilton, Concourse Level), 9am-12pm

Nathan Sleeter of *RRCHNM,* Brenda J. Santos of *Brown University,* Katharina Matro of *Walter Johnson High School,* Bob Bain of *University of Michigan,* *Ann Arbor*.

##### [Sinclair Workshop on Historical Podcasting: Panel Discussion](https://aha.confex.com/aha/2025/webprogram/Session26400.html)

Gramercy West (New York Hilton, Second Floor), 9am-10:30am

Chaired by Daniel J. Story of *University of California, Santa Cruz,* Co-lead by James Ambuske of *RRCHNM,* with presentations by Kathryn Carpenter of *Princeton University,* Andrew J. Falk of *Christopher Newport University,* Jessica Lynne of *Momus,* Natalia Mehlman Petrzela of *New School*.

##### [Sinclair Workshop on Historical Conversations: Breakout Conversations](https://aha.confex.com/aha/2025/webprogram/Session26401.html)

Concourse G (New York Hilton, Concourse Level), 11am-12:30pm

Chaired by James Ambuske of *RRCHNM,* with papers by Kathryn Carpenter of *Princeton University,* Andrew J. Falk of *Christopher Newport University,* Jessica Lynne of *Momus,* Natalia Mehlman Petrzela of *New School,* Daniel J. Story of *University of California, Santa Cruz*.
