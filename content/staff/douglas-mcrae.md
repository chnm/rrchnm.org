Douglas McRae is a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the Roy Rosenzweig Center for History and New Media. At RRCHNM, Douglas coordinates educational outreach and support for the digital research tool Tropy in the Americas. Douglas is an urban environmental historian, and has researched the history of water and sanitation in São Paulo, Brazil. Douglas holds a BA from Middlebury College an MA from Georgetown University in Latin American Studies, and a PhD in History from Georgetown University. He is a Returned Peace Corps Volunteer (Peru 2008-2011).
