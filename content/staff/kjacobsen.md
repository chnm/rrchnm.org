Kristin Jacobsen is the Digital Project Coordinator for the Arnhem Postal History Project. The Arnhem Postal History Project will make available mail and postcards from the Netherlands in the period 1939 and 1945. The collection will offer an understanding of the everyday lives of the Dutch during World War II, postal history and the holocaust. She has an MA in Applied History from George Mason University and a MMAS in Military History from the US Army Command and General Staff College.
